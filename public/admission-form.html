2. Enrollment Process and Fee Structure

2.1 Admission and Eligibility Criteria

The Prime Classes has structured its programs to support academic growth at each level, beginning with foundational learning and progressing to rigorous exam preparation. Eligibility requirements are tailored to ensure that students enter programs that align with their academic level and goals, maximizing their potential for success.

Eligibility Requirements:

  • Classes 3 (Pre-Foundation Program) : The Pre-Foundation Program is open to students in Classes 3 and 4. It emphasizes fundamental learning across core subjects, fostering early academic skills and preparing students for a gradual transition into competitive exam preparation.
  • Class 4 (Foundation Program) : Students entering Class 5 are eligible for the Foundation Program, where they begin structured preparation for competitive exams. This program introduces more focused learning in subjects critical to entrance exams, building the groundwork for advanced study.
  • Classes 6 (Junior Program) : Students in Classes 6 and 7 are eligible for the Junior Program. Here, instruction becomes more intensive, focusing on critical subjects and examination strategies that equip students with skills for exams like Sainik Schools, Rashtriya Military Schools, and RIMC.
  • Class 8 (Senior Program) : The SeniorProgram caters to students in Class 8, providing comprehensive training for competitive entrance exams like AISSEE ,R.M.S. This program includes detailed coursework, strategic study plans, and targeted assessments, preparing students for high-level exams and future academic challenges.
  • Class 7 (RIMC) : The RIMC Program at The Prime Classes is designed specifically for students in Class 8, focusing on preparing them for the highly competitive Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC) entrance exam. This program provides an intensive learning experience, strategically equipping students with the skills and knowledge necessary to meet the unique demands of the RIMC selection process.

This structure allows The Prime Classes to deliver specialized education that matches students’ academic stages and ensures a progressive build-up of knowledge, skills, and discipline.

2.1.1 Required Documents

The Prime Classes requires certain documents for student verification and record-keeping, ensuring that all enrolled students meet the required criteria for admission.

Documents Required for Admission :

  • Birth Certificate : Validates the student’s age eligibility for the program.
  • Aadhaar Card : Provides a verified identity document for each student.
  • Parent’s Employment Proof (for defense categories): For students applying under defense-related categories, a certificate or identification of the parent’s employment in the defense sector.
  • Residence Certificate (Nivas Praman Patra) : Confirms the student’s local residence, necessary for residency requirements in specific admission categories.
  • Domicile Certificate (Mul Nivas Praman Patra) : Verifies the family’s state of residence, applicable to certain admission requirements.
  • Ration Card (optional): Serves as an additional proof of residence and family identification.
  • Photographs : Two to four recent passport-sized photographs.
  • Previous School Marksheet : Required to confirm academic history and successful completion of the previous class.

Each document is essential to complete the enrollment process and ensure compliance with educational standards and program eligibility.

2.1.2 Admission Steps and Deadlines

The Prime Classes ensures that the admission process is straightforward, transparent, and efficient, allowing parents and students to complete the necessary steps with guidance from our admissions team.

Step-by-Step Admission Process :

  1. Initial Registration : The first step in the admission process is to complete the registration form. This step secures the student’s place for 7 days, allowing families the opportunity to evaluate the program before finalizing enrollment.
  2. Document Submission and Initial Payment : Once registration is complete, the documents outlined in Section 2.1.1 should be submitted for verification. In addition to document submission, an initial portion of the admission and tuition fees is required to confirm the student’s enrollment.
  3. Exam-Specific Application Deadlines : Students preparing for specific entrance exams such as Sainik School, RIMC, or NDA may have additional application deadlines set by government authorities. The Prime Classes provides support with filling out and submitting these forms promptly to ensure compliance with deadlines.

All necessary forms, documents, and fees must be completed by the given deadlines to secure the student’s place and begin instruction as scheduled.

3. Batch Assignment and Transfer Policy

Introduction The Prime Classes has a systematic approach to batch assignment and transfer to create a conducive learning environment for every student. With a structured system that divides students into batches based on age, academic level, and goals, The Prime Classes aims to place each student in a batch that best supports their educational journey. We have batches designed for foundational studies (Pre-Foundation and Foundation), junior-level students (TPC01 to TPC08), senior-level students preparing for 9th-grade entrance exams (TPC09, TPC10, TPC11), and specialized preparation for Rashtriya Indian Military College (RIMC1 and RIMC2). Each batch is tailored with specific schedules, including morning, full-day, and evening sessions to maximize flexibility and accommodate diverse student needs.

The following sections provide a detailed explanation of how initial batch assignment and transfer processes are conducted, ensuring transparency and fairness in creating a supportive academic environment.

3.1 Initial Batch Assignment

3.1.1 Criteria for Batch Placement

Batch assignment is carefully decided based on various factors, which help in categorizing students into the most appropriate group.

3.1.2 Notification of Batch Assignment

Once the batch is assigned, notifications are sent to parents and students via WhatsApp and are also available on the academy’s app. This ensures transparency, allowing families to understand the batch placement, session times, and other details, enabling a smooth start to the student’s learning journey.

3.2 Transfer Between Batches

The transfer policy allows flexibility in moving students between batches if a different batch would better suit their academic needs, learning pace, or schedule.

3.2.1 Eligibility for Transfer

Transfer eligibility is based on several factors:

  • Academic Progress : If a student demonstrates significant improvement, showing readiness for more advanced material, they may be eligible for transfer to a higher batch. Conversely, students needing extra support might be placed in a batch with more focus on foundational skills.
  • Suitability of Schedule : Students with changing availability (morning, full-day, or evening sessions) can request transfers to batches with more suitable timing. This ensures they can balance academic commitments with personal responsibilities.
  • Learning Pace and Intelligence Level : Regular assessments provide insights into a student's learning pace. Transfers may be recommended if a different batch better aligns with the student's needs, allowing them to benefit from an environment that matches their academic capacity.
3.2.2 Transfer Application Process

The process for requesting a transfer is straightforward, ensuring parents and students can access the option easily.

3.2.3 Conditions for Approval

The conditions for transfer approval include:

  • Suitability for the New Batch : A transfer is approved if the new batch environment aligns with the student’s goals, learning pace, and progress.
  • Weekly or Monthly Test Performance : Performance in assessments helps indicate readiness.
  • Behavioral and Attendance Record : Consistent attendance and positive behavior are considered.

Final Transfer Decision The transfer decision is a collaborative effort between the batch head and academic head, considering the student’s strengths, performance, and academic needs.
